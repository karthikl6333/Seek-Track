Travel Quest

Run through a country with the explorer, pocket the souvenirs, and earn a passport stamp.

How to play
  • Jump over holes, puddles, scooters, and water buffalo. Hold the button to jump higher.
  • Duck under shop signs and hanging lanterns.
  • Red flags are checkpoints. If you run out of hearts, try again from the last flag with your score still there.
  • Collect mango sticky rice, orchids, elephant tokens, banh mi, lanterns, and conical hats. Rare ones score more.
  • You have three hearts. A bump costs one. Hearts left at the gate add bonus points.
  • Finishing earns one star. A careful, high score earns two or three.

Keyboard: Space or up to jump, down to duck, P to pause, M to mute, R to restart. On the map, press 1 or 2 to start an open country. Vietnam unlocks after Thailand is stamped.
