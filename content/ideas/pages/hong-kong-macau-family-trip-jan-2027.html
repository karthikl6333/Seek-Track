Day 1 of 9 · Sat 9 Jan

Arrive, birds, fish and Ladies' Market

Arrive, then birds, goldfish and a stroll through Ladies' Market, ending with a vegetarian Indian dinner on Mody Road.

AnimalsShoppingIndian food
9:40 AM

Land at HKG on Cathay nonstop

Hong Kong International Airport is on Lantau island, about 35 km by road from the hotel (approx). Airport Express to Kowloon, then taxi to the hotel (Airport Express is HK$105 adult, HK$52.50 child). A cheaper option is the Citybus A21 to the Mody Road stop, see Taxi fares. Drop bags and rest.

What to do there
  1. Have passports and the printed Pre-arrival Registration slips ready at immigration
  2. Buy a Tourist Octopus card for MTR, trams and buses
  3. Ask the hotel to hold bags if the room is not ready
Afternoon

Kowloon Park aviary and Discovery Playground

A large public park in the middle of Tsim Sha Tsui with a walk-in aviary of parrots and other birds, ponds and a big children's playground. Flamingos and parrots. Free, aviary closes 5:45 PM.

What to do there
  1. Walk through the aviary and let her name the birds she spots
  2. Spend half an hour at the Discovery Playground to burn off the flight
  3. Walk the pond paths on the way out
3:30 PM

Mong Kok animal and market walk

Three neighbouring Mong Kok sights: a garden street where songbirds are kept in small cages, a street of shops displaying goldfish in plastic bags, and the Tung Choi Street market of clothes, toys and souvenirs. Yuen Po Bird Garden before 4 PM, the Goldfish Market, then Ladies' Market once the street goes pedestrian at 4 PM. Shopping slot.

What to do there
  1. Watch the bird keepers and the hanging cages at Yuen Po
  2. Count the colours of fish on Goldfish Market street
  3. At Ladies' Market choose one souvenir and practise a polite counter-offer
Dinner

Woodlands or Sangeetha, Mody Road, TST

Two vegetarian Indian restaurants a few minutes on foot from the hotel. Pure vegetarian Indian. Woodlands thalis are HK$129 (South Indian), HK$142 (North Indian) and HK$183 (Special), lunch 11:30 AM to 3:30 PM and dinner 6:00 to 10:30 PM.

What to do there
  1. Order a South Indian thali (HK$129) or a North Indian thali (HK$142)
  2. Ask for the child's portion to be mild
  3. Walk back along Mody Road to the hotel
Optional swap: Hong Kong Science Museum

Instead of the Mong Kok walk, go to the Science Museum a few minutes from the hotel. Open to 9:00 PM on Saturday. Arrive about 4:00 PM for the 5:00 PM Energy Machine. HK$20 adult, HK$10 concession.

Keep in mind

  • You land at 9:40 AM after a 5 hr 5 min flight. Hong Kong is only 2.5 hours ahead of India, so keep the first afternoon light.
  • Carry passports (valid 6+ months), the printed Pre-arrival Registration slips and some HKD cash for market stalls.
  • Kowloon Park aviary closes 5:45 PM. Yuen Po Bird Garden is best before 4 PM. Ladies' Market is pedestrian-only from 4 PM.
  • January is cool, about 15 to 19 C, so carry a light jacket.
  • Expect a lot of walking. MTR from East Tsim Sha Tsui to Prince Edward is HK$5.90 adult and HK$3.20 child if small legs tire.
  • Markets are crowded. Agree on a meeting point and hold her hand.

Day 2 of 9 · Sun 10 Jan

Pink dolphins and Harbour City

A boat trip to look for pink dolphins, then shopping at Harbour City.

AnimalsKid favouriteShopping
8:50 AM

Hong Kong Dolphinwatch boat trip

A boat trip to waters north of Lantau to look for Chinese white dolphins, locally called pink dolphins. Sightings are never guaranteed. Pickup outside The Kowloon Hotel, TST. Back around 1 to 2 PM. Book ahead by email. HK$580 adult, HK$380 child.

What to do there
  1. Scan the water from the deck and keep a family sighting tally
  2. Ask the guide how the dolphins are monitored
  3. Take photos of Lantau and the airport coast from the boat
Afternoon

Rest, then Harbour City and Ocean Terminal

A very large waterfront mall complex in Tsim Sha Tsui with children's and toy shops, plus the Ocean Terminal deck facing the harbour. Toy shops and kids' brands, waterfront views. Open 10 AM to 10 PM. Shopping slot.

What to do there
  1. Let her pick one toy or book inside a set budget
  2. Walk out to the Ocean Terminal deck for harbour views
  3. Have a snack break and rest before the evening
8:00 PM

A Symphony of Lights from the TST promenade

A free nightly light and music show on the Hong Kong Island skyline, watched from the Tsim Sha Tsui waterfront promenade. Free, about 10 minutes.

What to do there
  1. Arrive about 10 minutes early to find a spot at the railing
  2. Watch the buildings light up in time with the music
  3. Stroll the promenade back toward the hotel
Backup

LEGOLAND Discovery Centre at K11 MUSEA if the boat doesn't run

An indoor LEGO play centre in the K11 MUSEA mall in Tsim Sha Tsui. An indoor option for a rainy or rough-sea day.

What to do there
  1. Join a hands-on brick building zone
  2. Check the centre's website for rides and shows before going
  3. Book tickets online ahead
Optional swap: Hong Kong Space Museum

Around 3:00 to 5:00 PM, catch a dome show and the halls, then walk to the promenade for Symphony of Lights at 8:00 PM. Halls HK$10, show HK$40 or 30. Open 10:00 to 21:00 on Sunday.

Keep in mind

  • Dolphinwatch pickup is 8:50 AM outside The Kowloon Hotel, TST. Booking is by email and must be done well ahead.
  • Rough seas can cancel the boat. Carry seasickness tablets, a hat, sunscreen and a windproof layer.
  • The trip returns around 1 to 2 PM. Eat a light breakfast and pack water and snacks.
  • Harbour City is open 10 AM to 10 PM. It is large, so fix a shop list to save legs.
  • Symphony of Lights is at 8 PM and is free. Show times can change in bad weather, so check on the day.
  • Sunday crowds are heavy at the mall and promenade. Hold her hand.

Day 3 of 9 · Mon 11 Jan

Ngong Ping 360 and Citygate Outlets

Ride the Ngong Ping 360 cable car to the Big Buddha, then shop at Citygate Outlets.

Kid favouriteShopping
10:00 AM

Cable car to Ngong Ping and the Big Buddha

A scenic cable car ride over the sea and Lantau hills to Ngong Ping Village, home of the Tian Tan Buddha statue and Po Lin Monastery. Check the maintenance calendar in December. HK$295 adult, HK$150 child.

What to do there
  1. Pick a spot by the glass for views of the sea and the airport
  2. Climb the steps to the Tian Tan Buddha and look up close
  3. Walk Ngong Ping Village and the monastery grounds, and find a vegetarian snack
Afternoon

Citygate Outlets, Tung Chung

An outlet mall with discounted brand shops next to the Tung Chung MTR station and the cable car terminal. Two minutes from the cable car. Open 10 AM to 10 PM. Shopping slot.

What to do there
  1. Shop for children's and sports brands
  2. Compare prices before buying
  3. Have lunch at the mall and rest before the ride back

Keep in mind

  • The cable car can close for annual maintenance or in high winds. Recheck the maintenance calendar in December.
  • Start by 10 AM to avoid queues. Book tickets online if the site offers it.
  • Lantau hills are cooler and windier than the city. Carry a jacket.
  • There are steps up to the Buddha. Pace it for a child.
  • Citygate Outlets is open 10 AM to 10 PM, so you can shop after the cable car.
  • The ride from the hotel to Tung Chung is long, about 30 km by road (approx), so pack snacks.

Day 4 of 9 · Tue 12 Jan

Hong Kong Disneyland

A full day at Hong Kong Disneyland.

Kid favourite
Full day

Hong Kong Disneyland

Disney's Hong Kong theme park on Lantau, with a castle, family rides, character meetings and live shows. Buy tickets, then reserve the park date. Use the app for show and parade times. HK$1,837 to HK$2,583 for 3, by date tier. Tue 12 Jan is listed as Tier 1.

What to do there
  1. Go to the family rides first thing and save shows for midday
  2. Meet characters and use the app for wait and show times
  3. Finish with the castle show or parade if it runs that day
Closures to remember

The Space Museum is closed today (Tuesday). The Science Museum is open, but today is Disneyland.

Keep in mind

  • Tickets are tiered by date. The tier for 12 Jan comes from a third-party calendar, so confirm it on the official calendar when you buy.
  • Buy tickets and reserve the park date before you go. Entry needs a reservation.
  • Take the MTR to Sunny Bay, then the Disneyland Resort line (HK$22.20 adult, HK$11.10 child from East TST).
  • Carry a power bank for the app, a light jacket, water and snacks. Look for vegetarian options at park restaurants and check with staff.
  • Rides have height limits, so check before queuing.
  • Check park closing time and the parade or fireworks schedule in the app that morning.

Day 5 of 9 · Wed 13 Jan

Ocean Park

Pandas and sea lions at Ocean Park, then an easy thali dinner near the hotel.

AnimalsKid favouriteIndian food
10:00 AM

Ocean Park, pandas and sea life

A marine-themed park on the south side of Hong Kong Island with giant pandas, aquariums, animal feedings and rides. Catch the free sea lion and fish feedings. Penguin Expedition is paused until March, and the paid panda session is HK$1,880 a head, so skip it. HK$538 adult, HK$269 child.

What to do there
  1. See the giant pandas at the panda habitat
  2. Catch the free sea lion and fish feedings
  3. Ride the cable car or train between the park's two areas and try a few family rides
Evening

Easy night near the hotel

A rest evening after a full day on your feet. Early dinner on Mody Road.

What to do there
  1. Have an early thali at Woodlands or Sangeetha
  2. Pack and charge phones for tomorrow
  3. Take a short promenade stroll if energy allows

Keep in mind

  • MTR goes direct from East TST to Ocean Park station (HK$13.20 adult, HK$6.50 child). The park gate is right by the station.
  • Penguin Expedition is paused until March, so do not plan around it.
  • Skip the paid panda session at HK$1,880 a head. The free viewing is enough.
  • Check the day's feeding and show times at the gate and plan the day around them.
  • It is a hilly, large park. Wear comfortable shoes and pack a layer.
  • Check closing time on the day.

Day 6 of 9 · Thu 14 Jan

Zoo, trams, Peak and Temple Street

The zoo, the trams and the Peak by day, Temple Street Night Market by night.

AnimalsShopping
9:00 AM

Hong Kong Zoological and Botanical Gardens

A compact public zoo and garden on a hillside in Central. Free. Monkeys, birds and reptiles in Central.

What to do there
  1. Spot the monkeys and the birds
  2. Visit the reptile area
  3. Stroll the gardens before the heat of the day
Late morning

Ding Ding tram ride, then the Peak Tram and Sky Terrace

Hong Kong's double-decker trams run along the north shore of the island. The Peak Tram is a steep funicular up to Victoria Peak and the Sky Terrace viewing deck. Peak combo HK$182 adult, HK$118 child. Tram fare is HK$3.30 adult, HK$1.60 child per ride.

What to do there
  1. Sit upstairs at the front of the double-decker tram
  2. Ride the Peak Tram and look over the skyline from the Sky Terrace
  3. Take a short flat walk around the Peak
2 to 5 PM

Rest at the hotel

A planned rest to protect the evening. Break before the night market.

What to do there
  1. Nap or read
  2. Light snack
  3. Check the next day's ferry time
6:00 PM

Temple Street Night Market

A street market in Yau Ma Tei with souvenir and goods stalls, street food and fortune tellers. Street food and stalls, closes around midnight. Shopping slot.

What to do there
  1. Choose a small souvenir
  2. Try vegetarian snack stalls and check ingredients
  3. Watch the fortune-teller stalls near the temple

Keep in mind

  • The Peak queues grow by late morning. Go early or buy the combo ticket (HK$182 adult, HK$118 child) ahead.
  • The Peak is cooler and windier than the city. Carry a jacket.
  • Taxis to Hong Kong Island pay harbour tunnel tolls, so MTR to Central is much cheaper.
  • The zoo is on a slope. Expect uphill walking.
  • Temple Street comes alive in the evening and closes around midnight. Watch pockets and keep your daughter close.
  • Keep the 2 to 5 PM rest so she is fresh for the night market.

Day 7 of 9 · Fri 15 Jan

To Macau, House of Dancing Water

Travel to Macau and settle in at the Sofitel, then The House of Dancing Water.

Kid favouriteShopping
Morning

TurboJET ferry, Sheung Wan to Macau

A fast catamaran from the Hong Kong-Macau Ferry Terminal in Sheung Wan to Macau. About 60 minutes. About HK$564 for 3, weekday.

What to do there
  1. Keep passports handy for immigration at both ends
  2. Sit together and stow bags in the racks
  3. Watch the harbour and bridges from the window
Afternoon

Check in, stroll Ruins of St Paul's and Senado Square

The Ruins are the carved stone front of a 17th-century church, part of Macau's UNESCO-listed historic centre. Senado Square is the wave-patterned pavement square nearby. Shopping slot.

What to do there
  1. Climb the steps at the Ruins for photos
  2. Walk Senado Square's wave pavement
  3. Sample almond cookies from the nearby shops
7:30 PM

House of Dancing Water, City of Dreams

A large water-stage show with acrobats and divers, performed at City of Dreams. Ages 6+. Book ahead. MOP 698 to 1,598 per seat.

What to do there
  1. Arrive about 30 minutes early
  2. Choose a seat tier by view and budget
  3. Carry a light layer for the theatre
If you want teamLab on this day

teamLab SuperNature in Cotai is possible after arriving in Macau, but with the move and the evening show it is tight, so Sat 16 Jan is the better slot.

Keep in mind

  • Carry passports. Macau needs a return ticket, hotel booking and proof of MOP 5,000 per person.
  • Use MOP in Macau. Many places also accept HKD but check.
  • House of Dancing Water suits ages 6+ and sells out, so book ahead. A flash sale runs 28 Oct to 6 Nov 2026 for shows from 29 Oct 2026 to 31 Mar 2027.
  • Check the show start time and the day of the week for tickets.
  • The Ruins are on a slope with steps and crowded by afternoon.
  • Keep taxis for luggage legs (ferry to hotel) and use feet for Senado and the Ruins.

Pandas and Taipa Village

Giant pandas at the Panda Pavilion, then Taipa Village and a thali dinner.

AnimalsShoppingIndian food

Giant Panda Pavilion, Coloane

Macau's panda house, in the Seac Pai Van park on Coloane. Free. 10:00 to 13:00 and 14:00 to 17:00.

What to do there
  1. Watch the pandas through the viewing windows
  2. Walk the Seac Pai Van park around the pavilion
  3. Use the morning session, when the pavilion is quieter
Lunch

Taipa Village and Rua do Cunha

The old village streets of Taipa with Portuguese-style buildings, snack shops and souvenir stores. Snacks, souvenirs, Taipa Houses. Shops until about 9 PM. Shopping slot.

What to do there
  1. Walk Rua do Cunha and browse the snack shops
  2. Pick up almond cookies or peanut candy
  3. Look at the colourful Taipa Houses
Late afternoon

Rest, or Macao Science Center as a swap

A science museum with hands-on exhibits. Exhibition MOP 50.

What to do there
  1. Try the hands-on exhibits
  2. Check the website for any extra shows
  3. Or rest back at the hotel
Dinner

Woodlands Macau or Taal

Indian restaurants on the Macau peninsula. Both on the peninsula. Woodlands Macau thalis are MOP 118, 128, 138 and 158.

What to do there
  1. Order a thali at Woodlands Macau
  2. Share a dessert
  3. Be back in the hotel early for the final day
Optional swap: teamLab SuperNature Macao

After the pandas and a Taipa lunch, spend about 2 hours at teamLab in Cotai (MOP 288 adult, 208 child, MOP 784 for three). Last entry 6:15 PM. Closed Wednesdays in the 2026 schedule, January 2027 dates to be confirmed.

Keep in mind

  • Panda Pavilion is free and open 10:00 to 13:00 and 14:00 to 17:00. Check the Macao tourism page for closure days.
  • Taxis to Coloane carry a surcharge, see Taxi fares.
  • Taipa Village shops run until about 9 PM.
  • Saturday brings crowds. Start with the pandas.
  • Taipa Village streets are walkable but busy. Keep a hand on her.
  • Plan only one major stop in the afternoon.

Hong Kong Science Museum

Hours
10:00 to 19:00 Mon, Tue, Wed and Fri. 10:00 to 21:00 Sat, Sun and public holidays. Closed Thursdays, so closed Thu 14 Jan.
Price
HK$20 standard, HK$10 concession, free every Wednesday (13 Jan). Under 4 free with a paying adult. For you two plus your daughter that is about HK$50 on a normal day if she gets the concession (carry her passport or school ID; whether a foreign school ID is accepted is unconfirmed), or HK$60 if not.
Kid highlights
The Energy Machine, a giant kinetic sculpture that runs at 11:00, 14:00 and 17:00. The Children's Gallery for children up to 160 cm tall with an adult. Hands-on halls across several floors.
Time needed
2 to 3 hours.
Getting there
A few minutes' walk from the hotel, both in TST East (about 5 to 10 minutes, approx). Nearest MTR is East Tsim Sha Tsui.
Where it fits
Sat 9 Jan, in place of the Mong Kok walk after the aviary. It is open until 21:00 on Saturday, so arrive about 4:00 PM to catch the 5:00 PM Energy Machine, and keep dinner at Woodlands or Sangeetha afterwards. Tue 12 Jan is open too, but that is Disneyland day.
Check
I could not open the museum's admission page directly, so prices and hours come from its visit and opening-hours pages as returned by search. Confirm on the day, and check for any special closure.
Tsim Sha Tsui · swap for Sun 10 Jan

Hong Kong Space Museum

Hours
13:00 to 21:00 Mon and Wed to Fri. 10:00 to 21:00 on weekends and public holidays. Closed Tuesdays, so closed Tue 12 Jan (not Thursday).
Price
Exhibition halls HK$10, HK$5 concession, free on Wednesdays. Space Theatre dome shows HK$30 (front stalls) or HK$40 (standard stalls); concessions are about half, which I could not confirm for a child. A family of three: roughly HK$100 for a standard-stalls show plus HK$25 for the halls.
Kid highlights
The Stanley Ho Space Theatre has an OMNIMAX dome screen, and current 3D shows run about 28 minutes. The exhibition halls cover space science and astronomy with hands-on displays.
Showtimes
Show lists are published by the museum and change by season. The January 2027 times were not posted when I checked, so pick an afternoon session on the shows page once it is up.
Time needed
About 2 hours: one show plus the halls.
Getting there
About 1.2 km from the hotel (approx), a 15 to 20 minute walk along the harbourfront, or a short taxi. It is next to the promenade where Symphony of Lights is watched.
Where it fits
Sun 10 Jan afternoon, after the Dolphinwatch boat and a rest, around 3:00 to 5:00 PM, then walk to the promenade for the 8:00 PM Symphony of Lights and skip part of Harbour City.
Check
One of the museum's show-table pages looked older and listed Tuesday as open. The home, visit and LCSD pages all say closed Tuesdays, so I used that.
Cotai Expo, The Venetian · swap for Sat 16 Jan

teamLab SuperNature Macao

Hours
Daily 11:00 to 19:00, last entry 18:15. The official 2026 schedule shows Wednesdays dark, and the January 2027 schedule is not out yet. Sat 16 Jan is not a Wednesday, but confirm before you buy.
Price
MOP/HKD 288 adult and 208 child (age 3 to 12), so MOP 784 for the three of you. An optional EN TEA HOUSE add-on is MOP 58 per person on the ticket page. Another teamLab page says the tea house is July and August only, so treat the add-on as unconfirmed. Under 3 free. Up to 5 hours of free parking for ticket holders.
What it is
A 5,000 square metre, body-immersive set of digital art rooms with 8 metre tall works, on several levels. You walk in the art, and it responds to you. Includes the Floating Flower Garden room.
Is it right for her
Good for 8 year olds, who are interactive and can run around. Children under 13 must stay with an adult. The venue is dark with uneven or slippery floors, some mirrored floors (wrap skirts on request), strong light and loud audio. Wear closed flat shoes, no heels, flip-flops or sandals, and no large bags.
Time needed
About 2 hours. Outside food and drink is not allowed. No re-entry, flash photography is banned.
Where it fits
Sat 16 Jan afternoon: pandas in Coloane in the morning, a Taipa lunch, then a short taxi to Cotai Expo (about 10 minutes, approx), going in around 2:30 to 3:30 PM. It replaces the rest or Macao Science Center swap and some Rua do Cunha browsing. Fri 15 Jan is possible but tight after the move from Hong Kong and the evening show.
Booking tips
Buy online at Cotai Ticketing once January dates open (limit 8 tickets per purchase, no refunds unless cancelled). Check the schedule PDF for dark days first.
