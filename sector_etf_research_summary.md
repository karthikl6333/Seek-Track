## CRITICAL DISCLAIMERS

### Daily Reset Warning
All leveraged and inverse ETFs listed are **DAILY products** that reset exposure at the end of each trading day. They seek to achieve their stated leverage factor (2x, 3x, or inverse) **ONLY for a single trading day**, not for longer periods.

**Key Risks:**
1. **Compounding Effect:** Returns over multiple days will differ from the stated multiple due to daily compounding
2. **Volatility Decay:** High volatility can cause losses even when the underlying index is flat or moves favorably over time
3. **Not Buy-and-Hold:** These products are designed for short-term tactical trading, not long-term investing
4. **Potential for Total Loss:** A single-day move of >33% against a 3x fund can result in total loss

### Missing Products - Why?

**Space/Aerospace:**
- The sector is too small and illiquid for leveraged products
- SpaceX single-stock ETFs (LOFF/LOFD) exist but are NOT sector-wide products
- Traditional space ETFs (ARKX, UFO) are unleveraged only

**Infrastructure:**
- No issuer has created a dedicated infrastructure leveraged pair
- DUSL (Industrials 3x bull) is the closest proxy but lacks a bear counterpart
- Infrastructure is often mixed into industrials, utilities, and materials sectors

**Utilities Bear (3x):**
- Direxion offers UTSL (3x bull) but no matching 3x bear
- Only ProShares SDP (-2x) provides inverse utilities exposure
- Utilities sector volatility may be too low for 3x inverse products to attract sufficient trading volume
