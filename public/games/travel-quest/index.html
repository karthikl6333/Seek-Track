Travel Quest

Pick a traveller, run each city, and earn a passport stamp when a country is complete.

How to play
  • Jump over holes, puddles, scooters, and taxis. Hold the button to jump higher.
  • Duck under shop signs, lanterns, and banners.
  • Each country has three cities. Finish them in order. The last city stamps the passport and opens the next country.
  • Red flags are checkpoints. If you run out of hearts, try again from the last flag with your score still there.
  • You have three hearts. A bump costs one. Hearts left at the gate add bonus points.
  • Finishing earns one star. A careful, high score earns two or three.

Keyboard: Space or up to jump, down to duck, P to pause, M to mute, R to restart. On the map, press 1, 2, or 3 to start the next open city in that country. Choose Explorer, Dad, or Mom before the first run.
