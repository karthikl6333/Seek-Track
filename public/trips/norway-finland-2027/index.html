Day 1 of 7 · In transit

Mon 22 Feb: Depart India

Leave Hyderabad one day before the first hotel night and fly via a European hub towards Tromsø.

Travel dayPack smart
Night

Hyderabad (HYD) to a European hub

Fly via Frankfurt (Lufthansa) or Amsterdam (KLM) on one multi-city ticket. An overnight transit may be necessary depending on the published timetable.

What to do there
  1. Book HYD to TOS as one protected ticket
  2. Pre-book a vegetarian meal code on every sector
  3. Carry printed visa, insurance and hotel confirmations

Keep in mind

  • Prefer a single-ticket itinerary with protected connections; avoid separate-ticket self-transfers.
  • Keep warm layers, essential medicines, valuables and one change of clothes in cabin baggage.
  • Carry khakhra, thepla, roasted chana, nuts and ready-to-eat vegetarian food; check airline and customs rules for food imports.
  • Norway is 4 h 30 min behind India; Finland is 3 h 30 min behind.

Day 2 of 7 · Tromsø · Night 1

Tue 23 Feb: Arrive in Tromsø

Land at Tromsø, settle in at The Edge on the waterfront, and keep the first evening easy, with a romantic dinner above the city lights.

Honeymoon dinnerCity lightsEasy day
Arrival

Tromsø Airport (TOS) to the hotel

Airport Express bus NOK 125 one way per adult (2026 fare), or a taxi of about NOK 250–410 (estimate). The Edge is about 10 minutes from the airport.

What to do there
  1. Buy the bus ticket on board or in the app
  2. Check in at Clarion Hotel The Edge, Kaigata 6
  3. Ask reception to note your strict vegetarian breakfast needs
Afternoon

Harbour walk and city centre

Keep the day light. Sunrise 07:39, sunset 16:17, dark from about 17:20 (calculated for 23 Feb).

What to do there
  1. Walk the quayside by the hotel
  2. Storgata, the main shopping street
  3. Buy SIM or eSIM data if not already set up
Late afternoon

Arctic Cathedral or Fjellheisen, if time and weather allow

Arctic Cathedral NOK 90 at the door. Fjellheisen cable car NOK 645 return per adult, runs 09:00–00:00 and stops in strong wind.

What to do there
  1. Check Fjellheisen's live operating status first
  2. Ride up at blue hour for the best view
Evening

Romantic dinner at Fjellstua, then aurora only if energy allows

Fjellstua, the restaurant at the top of Fjellheisen, is open 09:00–23:30. Ask ahead for a strict vegetarian main. Do not make tonight the only aurora attempt.

What to do there
  1. Reserve a window table
  2. Watch the sky from the viewing deck if it is clear

Keep in mind

  • Breakfast at The Edge is included only on some rate types, so book a rate that includes it.
  • Pavements are icy in February; wear boots with grip or ice grips.
  • Typical February: about −9°C at night to −4°C by day (Weather Atlas averages), windy on the waterfront.

Day 3 of 7 · Tromsø · Night 2

Wed 24 Feb: Sámi reindeer and an aurora chase

Feed and sled with reindeer at a Sámi herding family's camp, then chase the northern lights after dark.

ReindeerSámi cultureNorthern lights
10:00 (4 h)

Sámi reindeer camp: feeding and sledding

Tromsø Arctic Reindeer, run by a Sámi herding family, with transport from Tromsø. 24 Feb is in their high season (13 Feb–7 Mar): Long sledding (25–30 min) NOK 2,350, Short sledding (10–15 min) NOK 2,090, Feeding only NOK 1,890 per adult. Free cancellation up to 24 h before.

What to do there
  1. Pick a package with feeding and, if you like, a sleigh ride
  2. Their standard lunch is reindeer stew, so request a strict vegetarian meal in writing
  3. Listen to joik (Sámi song) and stories in the lavvu
18:30 (5–7 h)

Guided Northern Lights chase

Small-group minibus chases run from about NOK 1,590 to 2,400 per person (see the activities catalog). Guides drive to clearer skies; sightings are never guaranteed.

What to do there
  1. Choose small group, or a private minibus for two
  2. Eat a proper dinner first; most tours give only snacks
  3. Confirm pickup point, weather-cancellation policy, photos and return time

Keep in mind

  • Confirm in writing: no meat, fish, seafood, meat broth, fish stock or animal-derived soup base.
  • Two long outings in one day is tiring. A gentler honeymoon swap: Night Reindeer Sledding (18:00, 4.5 h, three-course camp dinner and a chance of aurora, NOK 2,350 high season) tonight, and the aurora chase on 23 Feb.
  • Northern Horizon's campfire serves reindeer sausage; NorthernShots lists vegetarian and vegan options.
  • Aurora tours take 5–8 hours and return after midnight. Book with free cancellation where you can.

Day 4 of 7 · Rovaniemi · Night 3

Thu 25 Feb: Fly to Rovaniemi, Santa Claus Village

A short Finnair hop to Lapland, then the Arctic Circle line, Santa's office and the post office at Santa Claus Village.

Arctic CircleSanta Claus VillageIndian dinner
10:25–12:35

Finnair AY542 Tromsø to Rovaniemi

Schedule data shows AY542 on Thursdays from 26 Oct 2026 at 10:25–12:35 local time (A319, 1 h 10 min). Visit Rovaniemi lists the route as daily from 26 Oct 2026 to 28 Mar 2027. The PDF's 10:35–12:40 was the previous winter's Thursday timing.

What to do there
  1. Re-confirm on finnair.com before booking hotels or tours
  2. Check the checked-baggage allowance on your fare
  3. Clocks move forward one hour in Finland
Afternoon

Check in at Arctic City Hotel

Airport bus about €8 per person (pay the driver by card or cash), or a taxi of about €25–30. Check-in from 15:00, free luggage storage.

What to do there
  1. Drop bags and change into warm layers
15:30–18:00

Santa Claus Village and the Arctic Circle

Free entry. Santa Claus Office opens 09:00–18:00 daily from 4 Jan to 31 Mar 2027, and meeting Santa is free. Bus 8 from the centre is €3.60 one way. Sunset 17:13.

What to do there
  1. Step across the Arctic Circle line together
  2. Send a postcard from Santa Claus Main Post Office
  3. Couple photo at the Arctic Circle marker
Evening

Indian dinner and an aurora check

Curry Masala, Rovakatu 26, near the hotel: vegetarian tasting menu €33.95 per person (minimum two), open until 21:30. Then check the aurora forecast and look for a dark spot by the river if skies are clear.

What to do there
  1. Order dal, paneer and chana dishes
  2. Walk to the riverside away from street lights

Keep in mind

  • Typical February in Rovaniemi: about −13°C at night to −7°C by day (FMI 1991–2020 averages); clear nights can be much colder.
  • Taxis to Santa Claus Village cost about €20–32 (estimate); Taksi Rovaniemi charges €8 start plus €1.43 per km.

Day 5 of 7 · Rovaniemi · Night 4

Fri 26 Feb: Husky safari and Rovaniemi

Race through snowy forest behind a husky team, warm up in a private sauna, then a candlelit Lappish dinner.

HuskiesSaunaRomantic dinner
Morning

Guided husky safari

Bearhill Campfire Tour: 10 km driven by a musher, €206 per adult, runs 11 Jan–4 Apr 2027. Bearhill Call of the Wild: about 12–18 km driving your own team, 3 h, €249. Apukka Husky Adventure: 6 km self-drive, 2 h, €199–219 with city transfers.

What to do there
  1. Confirm taster ride or longer safari, and own sled or passenger
  2. Thermal outerwear is included on all three listed tours
  3. Ask about the kennel's animal-welfare practices
Afternoon

Arktikum museum or free time

Arktikum science centre and museum, €22 per adult. Keep the afternoon flexible rather than overloading the day.

What to do there
  1. Walk the Lumberjack's Candle Bridge and riverside
17:00–19:00

Sauna

Arctic City Hotel's shared sauna is free but split by gender (women 17:00–19:00, men 19:00–21:00). For a sauna together, book a private session for a fee, or a Junior Suite with its own sauna.

What to do there
  1. Book the private sauna at reception
Evening

Romantic dinner, then an optional aurora tour

Arctic Restaurant has a €48 vegetarian Light Menu that can be made vegan, open daily from 17:00. Optional: Aurora Hunting small-group van tour, €250 per person, 6–14 h, maximum 8 guests, full refund if no clear skies are found.

What to do there
  1. Reserve the dinner table ahead
  2. Book the aurora tour only if 24 Feb was cloudy

Keep in mind

  • This is your second aurora chance, not a guarantee.
  • Day length on 26 Feb is about 9 h 30 min (sunrise 07:44, sunset 17:16).

Check out and transfer to Apukka

Aurora Cabin check-in

Aurora Cabin: 24 m², heated panoramic glass roof, private shower and toilet, blackout curtains, electric fireplace, breakfast included. Winter clothing (overalls, boots, wool socks, mittens) is included for guests from 9 Nov to 4 Apr.

Private sauna and a vegetarian dinner

Sauna with outdoor jacuzzi €99 for two; Lakeside Sauna and Arctic Bath €199 for 1–2; Premium Finnish Sauna and Private Pier €269 for two; Sauna Carriage on skis at your door €126. Restaurant Aitta is open 07:30–23:00 and has vegetarian dinner options if told in advance.

Watch the sky from bed

The heated glass stays clear of frost. A glass roof does not guarantee an aurora sighting.

Paperwork

Schengen visa and insurance

Which country
Finland, as in the PDF: 3 nights in Finland and 2 in Norway, and you apply to your main destination (longest stay). Still Finland under fix A.
Where
Finland Visa Application Centre (VFS), Hyderabad, in person, by appointment
When
No earlier than six months before travel, so applications are open now. Processing is up to 15 working days and can stretch to 30 in high season; apply by early January to be safe (estimate).
Fees
Visa €90 (₹9,700) per adult plus VFS service fee €20 (₹2,151), paid by card, UPI or cash at the centre
Insurance
At least €30,000 medical cover, valid for the whole Schengen area and the whole trip, including repatriation
Documents
Passport, confirmed itinerary, accommodation and flight reservations, financial documents, employment and leave documents, travel insurance, plus the Finnish visa checklist
Pack for −15°C

Winter packing list

  • Thermal base layers, top and bottom, two sets each
  • Fleece or wool mid-layer
  • Insulated waterproof winter jacket and snow trousers
  • Insulated waterproof boots with good grip, plus ice grips
  • Wool socks and a warm hat or balaclava
  • Waterproof insulated gloves or mittens, plus thin liner gloves
  • Hand and foot warmers, moisturiser, lip balm, sunglasses
  • Power bank: phone batteries drain fast in the cold
  • Type C/F plug adapter (Norway and Finland)
  • Swimwear for saunas and the jacuzzi
  • Vegetarian snacks from India, within customs rules
  • Tour operators and Apukka lend thermal suits and boots; confirm before relying on it
